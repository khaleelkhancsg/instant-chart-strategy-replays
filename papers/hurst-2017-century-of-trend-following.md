## What it says

Extending time-series momentum (moskowitz-2012) to 67 markets in commodities,
equity indices, bonds and currencies from 1880, the authors find trend following
profitable in every decade, with low correlation to traditional assets and good
results during most of the worst stock-bond drawdowns. Performance varies with
the correlation between markets: diversified trend following does best when
markets are not moving together.

## How to use it here

The longest evidence that trends persist in futures -- at monthly horizons and
across many markets. Its relevance to an intraday single-market book is the
mechanism, not the numbers; and it is a reminder that a trend book's lean years
can last a long time, which a 2026-only evaluation cannot show.
