## What it says

The authors apply the universe of 7,846 technical rules from Sullivan,
Timmermann and White (filter, moving-average, support/resistance, channel
breakout and on-balance-volume families) to 5-minute data on the S&P 500 ETF
over 2002-2003, with bootstrap tests that account for the search. Before that
adjustment 42 rules are significant at 1%; after it, none of the 7,846 is
profitable, for the whole period or either year -- without trading costs, which
would only make it worse. Channel breakouts were the strongest family.

## Why it matters here

It is the closest published test to this project's setting: intraday rules on
the index MNQ tracks, at bar sizes the chart lab uses. Its verdict is the prior
any intraday rule found by search here must overcome -- and the reason every
search reports out-of-sample results and PBO.

## Caveats

Two years of data from 2002-2003; markets and microstructure have changed, and
a rule family that fails as a whole can still contain a robust, economically
motivated member.
