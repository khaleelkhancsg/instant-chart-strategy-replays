## What it says

The paper tests whether a mechanical opening range breakout rule, using only
public, historical information, beats a fair game: it measures the success rate
of the breakout trades and their returns against the benchmark of no
predictability. The authors' related work on crude oil futures reports returns
significantly above zero and success rates above a fair game.

## How to use it here

A second, independent ORB study (with zarattini-2023), framed as a test against
a fair-game benchmark rather than a buy-and-hold comparison -- the right framing
for an intraday rule. This project's own ORB result (it loses as the bot would
trade it, once bars crossing both levels are resolved realistically) is the
check any ORB claim must pass here.

## Caveats

Results are market- and period-specific; the crude oil findings come from the
authors' related papers rather than this one.
