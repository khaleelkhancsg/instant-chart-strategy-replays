## What it says

Park and Irwin review the theoretical and empirical literature on technical
trading. In their working-paper version, a majority of the modern studies they
classify report profits (58 of 92), more so in futures and currency markets
than in stocks, and less so in recent decades. They also catalogue the testing
problems most studies share: data snooping, choosing the rules after seeing
the data, and cost and risk assumptions that flatter the results.

## How to use it here

A map of what has been tried and the standard ways results mislead. The
failure modes it lists are the ones the MCP tools guard against: costs always
reported, search size counted, out-of-sample and overfitting figures with every
search.
