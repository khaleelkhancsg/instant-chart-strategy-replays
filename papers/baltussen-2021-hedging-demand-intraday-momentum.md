## What it says

Using intraday data on more than 60 futures markets from 1974 to 2020, the
return from the open to the last half-hour predicts the return over the last
30 minutes, and the effect partly reverses over the next days. The authors tie
it to hedging flows: option market makers short gamma and leveraged ETFs must
trade in the direction of the day's move near the close, which pushes prices
further the same way. The effect is stronger when net short-gamma exposure is
large.

## Why it matters here

It gives the intraday-momentum effect (gao-2018) a mechanism and extends it to
futures, including equity index futures -- MNQ's own market. Leveraged Nasdaq
ETFs (QQQ's 2x and 3x cousins) rebalance at the close, which is exactly this
flow.

## How to use it here

The test is the same as for gao-2018 (enter at 2:30 PM CT in the direction of
the day so far, exit by the 3:05 PM CT flatten), now with a reason to expect it
in MNQ; judge it on 2026 and across years, net of a tick a side.
